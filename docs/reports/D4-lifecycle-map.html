Lifecyclemap

The NBN software development lifecycle as a cycle of eight stages, opened to task level. Each stage box opens into its tasks; each task says who acts, what to do, the colour of the delegation decision, the gate, and what would change the colour. The twelve D3 modules are pinned to the rows they govern.

          The cycle

          Every stage opens red, generates amber or green, and closes on a red decision or gate. The dotted arrow from 8 back to 1 is 8.6 writing the learning back into the context 1.1 prompts from. Click a stage to open it. Stage colours were removed on 4 Sep because a stage banner adds nothing once the tasks carry colours; each box shows its profile and a tally of its task colours instead. Task 8.6 writes what production taught back into the context that Stage 1 prompts from, which is what makes this a cycle rather than a line.
